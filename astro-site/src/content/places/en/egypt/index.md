Start with the journey you want to have: city streets, river landscapes, ancient monuments or an unhurried stretch beside the sea.

## Find your own rhythm

A map can make everything look close. A good itinerary gives each place room to breathe. Choose an anchor for each part of your holiday, then leave space for the smaller moments between the headline sights.

Cairo, Luxor and Aswan tell different parts of the story. Sharm El Sheikh opens another chapter beside the Red Sea. Connect the places that interest you rather than trying to collect every name on a list.
