## Choose the experience, not just the photograph

A boat outing, a guided snorkelling session and a scuba dive are different activities. Read the actual description and ask the operator about the requirements of the experience you are considering.

Leave enough space in the week to enjoy your base. You do not need to turn every day into an excursion. The most useful plan balances the activities you care about with the rest you came for.
