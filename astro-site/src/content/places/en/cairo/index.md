A city to experience in layers: museum galleries, neighbourhood walks, busy markets and a quieter moment beside the Nile.

## Look beyond the famous silhouette

The Giza Pyramids belong on many first itineraries, but they need not define your whole city stay. A museum morning and a neighbourhood afternoon create a different picture from a day spent travelling between distant stops.

Choose one area at a time. Leave room to sit down, notice the architecture and enjoy a meal without watching the clock. Your route should make sense on the ground, not only as a list of attractions.

## Put the museum in context

The Grand Egyptian Museum can become a chapter of its own. Decide what you want from the visit before adding more stops around it: a few collections explored carefully often make a more lasting impression than a hurried sweep.
