THE 10 COMMANDMENTS OF FABIO

For an authentic and worry-free experience in Sharm El Sheikh, it's fundamental to know a few small but important rules. These rules were created to guide you through the local nuances.

1. Euro Coins: No!

Coins are not exchanged or accepted here. Bring only banknotes. Ensure you have small denominations for daily expenses.

2. SIM Cards: Official stores only

Buy only from authorized retailers like Orange or Vodafone to avoid surprises and ensure best coverage.

3. Taxis: Agreed price

Always agree on the price before or use our private service which guarantees fixed rates and comfort.

Avoid the 'Tourist Traps' from Mile One.

Taxis? No, thank you. Travel like an insider, not a target for overcharging. Seeking worry-free transport with fixed rates and absolute transparency? Trust Fabio’s private service: your peace of mind is priceless.

4. Antinal: The local remedy

For minor intestinal upsets, ask the pharmacy for Antinal. It's a very effective and well-known local 'lifesaver'.

Zero Surprises, Only Unforgettable Memories. Your peace of mind is my promise.

Wish to savor authentic local cuisine without a single worry? Keep my contact close at hand; consider me your dedicated 'guardian angel' in Sharm.

5. Dress Code: Respect and Class

Outside resorts, opt for discreet clothing out of respect for local culture. Inside, show off your vacation style with elegance.

Experience the City as an Insider, Not Just a Tourist.

Do you wish to uncover the city’s secrets with elegance and respect? Allow yourself to be guided by an expert who knows every hidden gem and secret corner.

6. Negotiation: A Fun Game

In souks and markets, bargaining is part of the experience. Do it with a smile; it's a way to interact with the local culture.

Experience Sharm as a Local, Not Just a Guest.

Seeking to uncover the city’s hidden gems with elegance and respect? Allow yourself to be guided by someone who knows every secret corner of this land.

7. Corals: Protect Nature

Corals are fragile ecosystems. Admire but do not touch or collect. You will help preserve the beauty of the Red Sea for everyone.

8. Baksheesh: Tipping Culture

Tipping is an integral part of the local culture to recognize good service. Offer it generously to express appreciation.

9. WATER: BOTTLED ONLY

Friendly advice? Avoid tap water at all costs. Stick to sealed bottles... unless you’re eager to meet your new best friend, Antinal!

10. RELAX: LEAVE THE CLOCK BEHIND

In Egypt, time has its own rhythm. Forget the rush and embrace the 'piano piano' philosophy. If things take a moment longer, meet it with a smile—it’s part of the authentic charm and local hospitality.

A Friend’s Advice: Those offering half-price steal your precious time in shops. With me, you pay for Freedom and for an impeccable service. Your holiday is an investment in moments, not in exhausting negotiations.

Must-See: Farsha Café. The Soul of Old Cairo.

Immerse yourself in the magical and timeless atmosphere of Farsha Café, a place where Egyptian history and culture blend into an unforgettable experience. This hidden gem, nestled among the rocks, offers breathtaking views of the Red Sea, surrounded by traditional decor and soft lights that create a unique ambiance. Enjoy a mint tea or a hookah, letting yourself be enveloped by local music and the sea breeze. It's more than just a café; it's a sensory journey into the heart of Egypt, a place where time seems to stand still and every moment becomes a precious memory.
